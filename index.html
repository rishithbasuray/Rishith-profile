About

Discipline on the court, curiosity off it.

I'm Rishith Basuray, a student with a passion for Economics, Mathematics and Basketball. I was selected by the Basketball Federation of India for its National Basketball Academy on a full scholarship, and I study at Corvuss American Academy on the US curriculum.

As a point guard and team captain, I've learned that leadership means reading the game, making others better and taking responsibility when it matters. Off the court, I enjoy economic theory, statistics and building things. That's how CourtBase started, my platform for verifying grassroots basketball statistics.

My goal is to study Economics at university while continuing to compete at a high level.

02 / Founder · from Oct 2026

Bengaluru Court Access Project

A regulation court costs around ₹7 lakh, which puts the sport out of reach for low-income communities. I'm mapping Bengaluru's public court access gap and designing a low-cost adjustable hoop that can be built locally, with a step-by-step build guide. Free play-first camps with NGO partners run from mid-November 2026 to mid-January 2027, and the hoops stay with the communities permanently.
